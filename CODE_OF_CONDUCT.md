# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where people can disagree about code without making it
personal, and where newcomers feel welcome to ask the obvious question.

We pledge to act in ways that keep Sonic a calm, useful place to contribute.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith.
- Criticise the code, not the author. Say what is wrong and why, and say what would be better.
- Accept that a maintainer may decide differently, and ask why before pushing back.
- Keep discussion about the project. Personal arguments do not belong here.
- Give credit. If someone's idea, report or fix shaped the work, say so.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, personal attacks, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or sustained disruption of a contribution.

## Scope

This applies in every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, and anywhere
someone is representing the project in public.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainer privately through
[GitHub's security advisory form](https://github.com/Abhiroop1122/runout/security/advisories/new).
Reports are handled confidentially. Reports about a security problem should go there rather than
into a public issue.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behaviour and are expected
to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour they consider
inappropriate, threatening, offensive or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code,
issues and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and to ban
temporarily or permanently any contributor for behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening,
offensive or harmful.
