The original glass listening room is back. The deck is the only player. 33/45, pitch and power controls are removed. Search cues songs on the platter.

## What changed

- Home is the original glass listening room: crate, picture disc, and glass turntable. There is no tab bar, mini player, or inked deck.
- The deck is the only in-app player. It has START/STOP, previous, next, the pixel screen, the tonearm, and flip.
- 33/45 keys, the pitch fader, and the power knob are gone. Records play at the speed they were recorded.
- Search does not play. Put on the platter cues the song with the needle up. START/STOP plays it.

## Install

Download **Runout-0.4.6-development.apk** and verify it with the attached SHA-256 file. It installs as **Runout Dev** (`app.runout.dev`) and updates the 0.4.5 development build when signed with the same certificate.

## Verification and limits

This is a debug-signed development pre-release, not a production release. No pull request is opened by this release.
