Runout 0.4.3 development adds the idle vinyl rack and the first inked home shell. It keeps FLAC-only playback, Soulseek, and Friday Drops.

## What changed

- When nothing is on the platter, Home is a full-screen row of records. Scroll up or down to move them. Tap the facing disc to unbox it, then drag it onto the platter.
- The home chrome follows the inked owner's manual: paper background, soft pills, Downloads, Settings, and Crate. A printed transcription deck is used once a record is in the room.
- This is not the finished gesture storyboard. Arm drag, turnover, and the full deck drawing are still ahead.

## Install

Download **Runout-0.4.3-development.apk** and verify it with the attached SHA-256 file. It supports Android 9+ and ARM64, ARMv7, x86-64 and x86. It installs as **Runout Dev** (`app.runout.dev`) and updates the 0.4.2 development build when signed with the same certificate.
